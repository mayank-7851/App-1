Careers at Kaapi

Help us bring extraordinary coffee from the Western Ghats to cups around the world. We're a small team doing big things — and we'd love to meet you.

Chikmagalur, Karnataka · Full-time

Head Roaster

Own the roast curve from green to cup. You'll profile every lot, manage our 15 kg roaster, and work directly with producers to bring out the best in each bean. Three or more years of production roasting required.

Bangalore, Karnataka · Full-time

Café Manager

Lead our flagship brew bar in Indiranagar. You'll hire and train baristas, dial in the daily pour, and create a space where regulars feel at home. Hospitality leadership experience is a must.

Remote / Chikmagalur · Contract

Green Coffee Buyer

Travel the Western Ghats to discover extraordinary lots from smallholder farms. You'll build relationships with growers, cup samples, and negotiate contracts. Q-grader certification preferred.
