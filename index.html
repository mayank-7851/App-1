Our Story

From the misty hills of Coorg to your morning ritual

Kaapi was born from a simple belief — that the slow, deliberate ritual of South Indian filter coffee deserves a place in the modern kitchen. For generations, families across the subcontinent have woken to the gentle burble of brass filters, the rich aroma of freshly decocted coffee mingling with the quiet of dawn. We're here to carry that tradition forward, one perfectly extracted cup at a time.

Our beans are sourced directly from smallholder farms in the Western Ghats — Coorg, Chikmagalur, and the Nilgiris — where coffee has been cultivated under dappled shade canopies for over a century. Each lot is hand-picked, sun-dried on raised beds, and roasted in small batches to honour the unique character of the terroir. No shortcuts, no compromises.

What reaches your cup is more than coffee. It's the patience of the pour, the warmth of shared tables, and the quiet confidence of a craft preserved across generations. This is Kaapi — slow, intentional, unforgettable.
