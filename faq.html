Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about our beans, shipping, and subscriptions — in plain English.

How soon will my coffee ship after I order?

We roast every Monday and Thursday. Orders placed before midnight on Sunday ship Monday; orders placed before midnight on Wednesday ship Thursday. Your coffee leaves our roastery within 48 hours of roasting — never sitting on a shelf. You'll get a tracking number the moment the label is printed.

How fresh are the roast dates? Do you roast to order?

Every bag carries a roast date stamp — not a "best by" guess. We roast in small batches to match demand, so the beans that land on your doorstep are typically 2–4 days off roast. That's right in the sweet spot: rested enough to degas, fresh enough to bloom beautifully in your brewer. Most coffee on supermarket shelves is weeks or months old — ours never is.

Do you offer subscriptions? Can I pause or cancel?

Yes — we offer weekly, fortnightly, and monthly subscriptions. Pick your favourite bean (or let us surprise you with a rotating single-origin), choose your grind size, and we'll keep it coming. You can pause, skip, or cancel anytime from your account page — no questions asked, no phone calls, no guilt. Try the Beans page to see what's currently in rotation.

Where do your beans come from?

All our coffee is single-origin from South India — primarily the hills of Chikmagalur, Coorg, and the Nilgiris. We work directly with smallholder farmers and cooperatives who pick only ripe cherry and process with care. Each lot is traceable to a specific estate or washing station. No blends, no filler, no mystery beans. Read the full story on our Story page.

What's the best way to store my coffee?

Keep it simple: an airtight container in a cool, dark cupboard — away from heat, light, and moisture. Not the fridge (condensation kills flavour) and not the freezer for daily use (freeze only what you won't touch for 3+ weeks). Our resealable bags have a one-way valve to let CO₂ out without letting oxygen in, so they're perfectly fine for 2–3 weeks after opening. For peak flavour, aim to finish a bag within three weeks of the roast date.
