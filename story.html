Our Story

Rooted in the hills of Chikmagalur.

Every cup of Kaapi begins its journey in the birthplace of Indian coffee — a story of land, care, and craftsmanship.

Where we come from

Chikmagalur, South India

Nestled in the Western Ghats of Karnataka at elevations between 900 and 1,500 metres, Chikmagalur is where coffee first took root in India. In the 17th century, Baba Budan planted seven beans brought back from Yemen — and from those seven seeds grew a legacy that now spans millions of acres across the region.

The district’s red laterite soil, steady monsoons, and cool mountain air create an environment that’s remarkably close to coffee’s Ethiopian highland origins. Arabica thrives here under the shade of silver oak, pepper, and cardamom — a polyculture that produces beans of uncommon complexity: notes of dark chocolate, jaggery, and stone fruit that define South Indian specialty coffee.

We work directly with a handful of family-run estates in the Baba Budangiri and Mullayanagiri ranges — farmers who’ve tended these slopes for generations. Our relationships are built on multi-year commitments and above-fair-trade pricing, because great coffee depends on thriving communities.

How we roast

Small-batch, every single day

We don’t roast until you order. It’s that simple. Every batch is sized so it moves through the drum with precision — never more than 15 kg at a time — which means our roaster can read each bean rather than babysit a machine.

Our Loring drum roaster gives us fine control over airflow and heat, but the real difference is attention. We roast to the bean, not to the clock. A natural-process Arabica from Chikmagalur might hit first crack at a slightly different moment than its washed counterpart; we listen, smell, and sample — profiling every lot individually.

Once a batch drops, it’s cooled immediately, rested for 24–48 hours to degas, then sealed in nitrogen-flushed bags and shipped. From roaster to your door within 72 hours. That’s the window where coffee peaks — and we’ve built every part of our process around hitting it.

  • Roasted to order, never warehoused
  • 15 kg max batch size for precise profile control
  • Nitrogen-flushed packaging locks in freshness
  • Shipped within 48 hours of roast
What we believe

Craft over volume, always.

Three principles that guide every decision we make — from sourcing to shipping.

Direct relationships

We buy from estates we’ve visited, not from commodity brokers. Multi-year commitments give farmers stability and give us traceability.

Peak freshness

We roast only after you order and ship within 48 hours. Coffee peaks 5–21 days post-roast — that’s when it should be in your cup, not on a shelf.

No corners cut

Every bean is hand-sorted after roasting. Defects that machines miss, we catch. It’s slower, but it’s the only way we’d serve coffee to someone we know.
